About these numbers

  • Every factor is an ETF proxy: long ETF daily return minus short/benchmark ETF daily return, compounded (daily rebalanced). Long-only smart-beta ETFs overlap heavily with SPY, so spreads are much smaller and noisier than true long-short factor portfolios.
  • Adjusted closes from Yahoo Finance (dividends reinvested). Yahoo re-adjusts history after each distribution; the refresh re-downloads a ticker's full history when that moves stored closes by more than 0.3%.
  • No look-ahead: z-scores use only the days before each date; today's bar is ignored until the US close is final (16:20 ET). A day missing for one ETF is folded into the next common day's return.
